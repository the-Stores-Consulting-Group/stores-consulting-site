## tSCG Software Support

If you use *the* Stores Consulting Group software and something is not working as expected, our team is here to help. Send us a support request and we will follow up with you directly.

## Submit a support request

Email [contact@storesconsulting.com](mailto:contact@storesconsulting.com?subject=tSCG%20Software%20Support%20Request) with the subject line **tSCG Software Support Request**. The more detail you include, the faster we can help.

Please include:

- Your name, company and a phone number or email where we can reach you
- The software and version or environment you are using (for example, production or test)
- A description of what happened and what you expected to happen
- The steps needed to reproduce the issue, and any error messages you saw
- Screenshots or files that help illustrate the problem
- How urgent the issue is and how many users or stores it affects

## Not a software issue?

For questions about our consulting services, or to start a conversation about your operation, use the [contact page](/contact-us/).
