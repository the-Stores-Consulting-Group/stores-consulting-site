Kroger is one of the largest supermarket retailers in the United States, founded in 1883 in Cincinnati, Ohio. The company operates roughly 2,800 stores across 35 states under nearly 20 different banners, including Kroger, Ralphs, Fred Meyer, Smith’s, King Soopers, Harris Teeter, and QFC. Kroger is publicly traded and reported fiscal year 2025 sales of $147.6 billion.
