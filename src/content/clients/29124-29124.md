Lunds & Byerlys is an upscale grocery chain with roots dating to 1922 in Minneapolis, Minnesota, unified under its current combined name in 2015. The company operates 27 stores under a single banner in the Minneapolis-St. Paul area. Lunds & Byerlys remains a privately held, family-run company headquartered in Edina, Minnesota.
