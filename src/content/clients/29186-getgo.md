GetGo (GetGo Cafe + Market) is a convenience store chain offering fuel, prepared food, and café service, founded in 1985 and headquartered in Cranberry Township, Pennsylvania. Long operated as a subsidiary of grocer Giant Eagle, GetGo operates roughly 270 locations across Pennsylvania, Ohio, West Virginia, and Maryland. In June 2025, Giant Eagle completed the sale of GetGo to Alimentation Couche-Tard (parent of Circle K) for $1.57 billion, and GetGo now continues to operate as a standalone brand under Couche-Tard ownership.
