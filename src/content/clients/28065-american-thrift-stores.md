America's Thrift Stores (also known as American Thrift Stores) is a for-profit thrift retailer founded in 1984. The company operates 17 stores across Alabama, Georgia, Louisiana, Mississippi, and Tennessee under a single banner, selling secondhand clothing, furniture, and household goods. America's Thrift Stores shares a portion of its net profits with local Christian ministries and charitable partners.
