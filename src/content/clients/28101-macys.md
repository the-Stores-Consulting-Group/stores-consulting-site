Macy's is an American department store chain whose brand dates to 1858, operating today as the flagship nameplate of publicly traded Macy's, Inc. The Macy's banner operates 432 locations, including department stores, small-format stores, and Backstage off-price locations. Macy's, Inc. also owns the Bloomingdale's and Bluemercury banners, bringing the parent company's total footprint to 663 stores across all three nameplates. Headquartered in New York City, Macy's, Inc. reported fiscal year 2025 net sales of approximately $21.8 billion.
