AML Foods Limited is a Bahamian grocery and food-service company founded in 1975 in Nassau. The company operates nine grocery and wholesale stores under banners including Solomon's, Solomon's Fresh Market, Cost Right, and its Family Islands markets in Exuma, Eleuthera, and Harbour Island, alongside roughly ten Domino's Pizza franchise locations. AML Foods is publicly traded on the Bahamas International Securities Exchange and is headquartered in Nassau, Bahamas.
