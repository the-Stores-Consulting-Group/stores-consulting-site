Tops Friendly Markets is a supermarket chain founded in 1962 in Niagara Falls, New York. The company operates approximately 150 stores, primarily in New York and Pennsylvania, along with 58 gas station and convenience locations, under a single banner. Tops is part of Northeast Grocery, Inc., sibling company to the Price Chopper/Market 32 banner, and remains headquartered in the Buffalo, New York area.
