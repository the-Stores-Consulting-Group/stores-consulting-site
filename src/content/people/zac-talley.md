Zac is a Managing Consultant with The Stores Consulting Group. Before joining tSCG, he spent 10 years in field and store-level roles with Dick’s Sporting Goods, Aldi, and Target. Since then, he has led Production Planning implementation projects on most of the leading software platforms on the market, including tSCG’s own Production Planning tool. In addition to Production Planning rollouts, he has led numerous Fresh foundational programs that help retailers build the operational and data groundwork needed to get the full return on their technology investment.
