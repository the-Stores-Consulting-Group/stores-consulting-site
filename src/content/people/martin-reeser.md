Martin Reeser leads the AI-Enabled Retail Operations practice at tSCG, helping grocery and food retail operators close the gap between the technology they have invested in and the store-level results they expected. Prior to tSCG, Marty served as Global VP of Retail Operations at Whole Foods Market (Amazon). He also served as RVP at gopuff, driving hypergrowth for e-com micro-fulfillment centers through the height of the pandemic. Marty served many of the Top 50 US food retailers with Deloitte's Retail & Consumer Practice, by putting into practice, what he learned by owning and operating a multi-unit supermarket business.
