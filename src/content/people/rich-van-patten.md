Rich is a Director with the Stores Consulting Group. He has over 18 years of large consulting firm experience and 23 years of industry experience working with a large regional grocer in the northeast, where he was primarily focused on store process improvements and Workforce Management. Rich was the Director of Operational Engineering, responsible for all aspects of Workforce Management at a large grocer, prior to his most recent experience in consulting. His consulting experience has had him working with many of the largest retailers in the world to solve their most unproductive issues. Rich spent the last 8.5 years with Accenture leading their Global Workforce Management offering, delivering complex WFM implementations. Rich graduated from the New School for Social Research with a BS in Human Resource Management. Rich recently retired from the US Air Force as a Lt. Col. with over 28 years of service flying LC-130H aircraft in the polar regions of the world.
